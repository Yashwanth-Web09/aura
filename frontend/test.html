The Alchemy Process

Horizontal Scrub
STAGE 01

Wild Banana Leaf Ferment

Fermented for 144 hours in native Spanish Elm cases beneath fresh jungle banana leaves to develop delicate jasmine notes.

STAGE 02

Granite Stone Conching

Cru crushed down to an extraordinary 10-micron fineness under Swiss granite stones for 72 uninterrupted hours.

STAGE 03

XO Cognac Cask Aging

Unpoured chocolate blocks rest for 180 days in virgin French oak barrels previously used to age 50-year Grande Champagne Cognac.

STAGE 04

Florentine 24K Gilding

Hand-gilded in our Zurich atelier using authentic Giusto Manetti Battiloro 24-karat gold leaves.
